# Public Communication Piece
The question we're evaluating is how does police reporting vary across the county?

In our public deliverable, we've put together an article detailing our research, findings, and open questions. We combine our research and visualizations with visualizations on the platform ArcGIS to map policing data on developmental tiers. 

## The workflow outside GitHub

We combined our figures and featurized our dataset to pull out longitude, latitude, reported vs. unreported crime, and time in order to plot the crime across developmental tiers on the ArcGIS mapping of Durham.
